How I Work

My Design Process

01
Understand

User interviews, workflow analysis, competitive audit. I don't open Figma until I understand the actual pain — not the assumed one.

02
Frame

Define success precisely. What metric moves? What's the simplest solution? I design for outcomes, not feature lists.

03
Design

Wireframes → high-fidelity → prototype. I design systems, not screens — tokens, components, states, edge cases.

04
Ship

I implement what I design — Angular, React, HTML/CSS in production. Then measure, learn, and iterate.

Philosophy
AI without UX
is just code.

Four principles I apply at the intersection of AI engineering and product design — the two disciplines I now practice together.

01

AI is only as good as its UX

A perfect model with a confusing interface fails the user. I design the interface that makes AI outputs trustworthy, understandable, and actionable.

02

ML decisions need design decisions

Which model output to show, how to communicate confidence, how to handle edge cases gracefully — these are design problems as much as engineering ones.

03

Design the edge case first

In AI products, the edge case is when the model is wrong or uncertain. Design for that first — empty states, low-confidence outputs, errors — and the happy path takes care of itself.

04

If I design it, I build it

Most designers hand off. I don't. I write the Angular, train the model, integrate the API, and ship it. No translation layer, no lost intent.
