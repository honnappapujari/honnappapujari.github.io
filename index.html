How I Work

My Design Process

01
Understand

User interviews, workflow analysis, competitive audit. I don't open Figma until I understand the actual pain — not the assumed one.

02
Frame

Define success precisely. What metric moves? What's the simplest thing that could work? I design for outcomes, not feature lists.

03
Design

Wireframes → high-fidelity → prototype. I design systems, not screens — tokens, components, states, edge cases, error flows.

04
Ship

I implement what I design — Angular, React, HTML/CSS in production. After launch: measure, learn, and iterate from real data.

Philosophy
Design is a verb,
not a title.

Four principles guiding every product decision — from Fortune 500 platforms to weekend side projects.

01

Friction is the enemy

Every extra click, every required signup, every confusing label reduces success. "No signup to start" was the highest-leverage decision in Reportly — I obsess over time-to-value.

02

Mental models beat feature lists

Users don't want a "classification dashboard" — they want to feel in control of compliance risk. The right framing changes what you build and how users succeed.

03

Design the edge case first

Empty states, errors, zero-data views — design these well and the happy path takes care of itself. Learned this building compliance workflows for security analysts.

04

If I design it, I build it

Most designers hand off. I don't. Angular, React, HTML/CSS in production. Faster iteration, more realistic designs, zero "can't build that" conversations.
